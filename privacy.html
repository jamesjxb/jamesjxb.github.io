Privacy Policy

James Hermes · Effective 3 November 2026

Who runs this app

James Hermes is a private, single-user tool. Its owner, GitHub user jamesjxb, runs it on his own computer for his own accounts only. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data it accesses

The owner grants access through Google's consent screen. The app then has read-only access to the owner's Gmail (the gmail.readonly scope). It searches and reads the owner's messages for his personal tasks. For example, it reads order and payment emails to keep his household accounts. It cannot send, delete or change email.

How the data is stored and used

  • Access tokens and any information taken from email are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Email content may be sent to an AI model provider, but only to carry out the owner's own requests.
  • The data is not sold or used for advertising. This app does not use it to train AI models.
  • The data is not shared with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

James Hermes uses and transfers information received from Google APIs in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Withdrawing access stops the app from reading any more email.

Contact

For questions, contact jamesjxb on GitHub.
